# 🚀 PM Validation Portal

A comprehensive Product Management validation, auditing, scoring, and maturity assessment platform built from the PM Fundamentals framework.

The PM Validation Portal transforms PM deliverables into measurable, reviewable, and executable artifacts by providing:

✅ Validation Checklists

✅ PM Maturity Scoring

✅ Radar Chart Assessments

✅ Traceability Validation

✅ Risk Reviews

✅ Go / No-Go Decisions

✅ Executive Dashboards

✅ PRD Quality Audits

✅ Research Quality Reviews

✅ Product Discovery Reviews

---

# Why This Exists

Most PM work is reviewed subjectively.

Questions like:

- Is this a good PRD?
- Is the research strong enough?
- Is the selected problem actually validated?
- Does the solution solve the root cause?
- Are the metrics meaningful?

Often have no consistent answer.

The PM Validation Portal introduces a repeatable framework that converts PM reviews into a structured process.
